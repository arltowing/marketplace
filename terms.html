Marketplace Terms

Seller and buyer usage terms for the MVP marketplace.

Seller Terms

Sellers must own or have rights to sell uploaded software packages. Sellers accept a 5% platform fee on gross sales. Payouts are tracked in the seller payout ledger and handled manually in the MVP phase.

Buyer Terms

Buyers receive access to approved digital downloads after successful payment confirmation.
